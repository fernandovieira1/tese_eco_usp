**Doctoral Researcher:** Fernando Souza da Vieira

**Advisor:** Prof. Dr. Alexandre Chibebe Nicolella

**Institution:** Faculty of Economics, Administration and Accounting of Ribeirão Preto (FEARP/USP)

**Research Period:** 2025-2028

---

## Overview

This repository gathers the materials for a PhD thesis proposal in Applied Economics at FEARP/USP. It is organized around the theoretical development of economic commensalism, together with the supporting code, empirical data, graphics, and draft chapters used throughout the project.
